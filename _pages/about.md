Hi! I'm a fourth year graduate student at Harvard University working with [Jesse Snedeker](https://www.harvardlds.org/jesse-snedeker/). My research asks how children understand language -- what goes on in kid's brains when they are hearing someone talking to them, or listening to a story? How do kids understand the words they are hearing? Lots of my research uses electroencephalography (EEG), a neuroimaging method that lets us record and analyze the electrical activity happening in the brain.
